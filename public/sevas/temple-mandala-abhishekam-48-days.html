About this Seva

A ceremony planned around your family

A series of sacred bathing rituals performed at a temple over a traditional 48-day mandala period, with offerings and abhishekam made to the deity with prayers.

Mandala Abhishekam is a series of sacred bathing rituals performed at a temple over a traditional 48-day mandala period. Offerings and abhishekam are made to the deity with prayers and devotional observances.

Share your preferred date, location, Muhurtham window, and any family or tradition notes. The coordinator will confirm priest availability, the final timing, and samagri arrangements with you.

Ceremony details

What is included

  • check_circle48 days of abhishekam and archana tharpanam
  • check_circleKalasha abhishekam and traditional naivedhyam
  • check_circleMandala pravachanam, kola purasabha, and prasadam

Traditional Significance

Purpose of the Ritual

Shared by devotees who request this seva, and offered as prayers during the ceremony.

  • check_circleTo offer sustained worship and prayers to the deity.
  • check_circleTo seek blessings for peace, health, and prosperity.
  • check_circleTo encourage devotion and spiritual discipline among participants.
  • check_circleTo bring the temple community together in worship over the mandala period.
