About this Seva

A ceremony planned around your family

Conducted for first birthdays or the birthdays of elders, with prayers for longevity (Ayur Vriddhi), vital energy, and protection from severe illnesses.

Sri Ayushya Homam is commonly requested for a child's first birthday or an elder's birthday. The family can share the person's name, age, preferred date, and any family custom so the coordinator can discuss the Sankalpam and ceremony plan.

The listed arrangements include invocation of Ayur Devata and Markandeya, Brahma-Varuna Kalasa Pooja, and Charu preparation support. Confirm the timing, samagri, and participation details for your chosen date.

Share your preferred date, location, Muhurtham window, and any family or tradition notes. The coordinator will confirm priest availability, the final timing, and samagri arrangements with you.
